02 / Setup

Run two programs.
Then you know.

Use a laptop or desktop. Pickcode runs in a browser, so most people will not need to install anything.

  1. 01

    Join the Pickcode class.

    The directions come by email and in Google Classroom. You’re ready when a circle moves down the window. Prefer the desktop editor? Install Processing ↗

  2. 02

    Download the Session 1 files. ↓

    Sketches, Java programs, a student handout, and a teacher guide.

  3. 03

    Run the plain-Java example. ↗

    Run plain-java/MovingBall.java from the download. You’re ready when it prints five frames.

Using a Chromebook, tablet, or school-managed device?

Use Pickcode, which runs in a browser, so it should work unless your school blocks it. The join link comes by email and in Google Classroom. Tell us if you cannot open it.

03 / Check in

Five answers.
One email.

These tell us who is joining live, who is working on their own, and who needs setup help before we start.

  1. 01

    Which dates can you join live?

    November 8, 15, 22, December 6, 13. Or none, if you are working on your own.

  2. 02

    How will you mostly take part?

    Mostly live, mostly on your own, or a mix.

  3. 03

    Where is your setup?

    Both run, Processing only, Java only, or neither yet.

  4. 04

    What computer will you use?

    The device and operating system, and whether it is school-managed.

  5. 05

    What help do you need?

    Setup, access needs, or anything else.
