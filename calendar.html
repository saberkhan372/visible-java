Pacing · five 50-minute periods a week

Every day, in order

140 class days over 28 weeks, from the first day of class to the AP exam, with the topic and the work for each one. There are no dates here, so lay it over your own school calendar: when your school is closed, the plan simply waits.

Fitting it to your year. Many schools have about 30 weeks of instruction before the May exam, so 28 leaves room for assemblies, testing, and snow days. Eight flex days are already in the plan. Spend them where you fall behind, or move them. Build lessons take two periods: day one is prediction and the first missions, day two is checks, AP transfer, and the exit slip.

AP Computer Science A Exam

May · check AP Central for this year’s date

Three hours. 42 multiple-choice in 90 minutes (55%), then four free-response in 90 minutes (45%). Digital, in Bluebook, with the Java Quick Reference on screen and no Run button.

After the exam

Whatever time is left in the year: a Unit 4 lab-choice project or a free build. The Processing extensions in each lesson are a good place to start.

Topics follow the AP Computer Science A Course and Exam Description effective Fall 2025. Pacing lives in assets/calendar.js: change a lesson’s days and every week and day number on the site updates.

This is a plan, not a promise. The pace of the room matters more than the row number.
